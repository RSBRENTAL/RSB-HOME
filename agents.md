# RSB Rental Scooter Barcelona

RSB Rental Scooter Barcelona is a local rental shop in Vila Olímpica del Poblenou, Barcelona, near Port Olímpic, Nova Icaria Beach and Barceloneta Beach.

## Contact

- Website: https://rentalscooterbarcelona.com/
- Address: Carrer de Salvador Espriu, 63, 08005 Barcelona, Spain
- Telephone and WhatsApp: +34 640 559 468
- Email: info@rentalscooterbarcelona.com
- Opening hours: every day, 10:30-13:30 and 16:30-20:00

## Rental services

- 50cc and 125cc petrol motor scooters
- Inline skates and Rollerblades
- Standard city bikes
- Classic quad roller skates
- Skateboards
- Longboards

Electric kick scooters and e-bikes are not listed as rental services.

## Customer information

- Free scooter hotel delivery applies to rentals of three or more days.
- Free luggage storage is available for rental customers.
- No deposit applies to bikes, inline skates, quad roller skates, skateboards and longboards.
- A refundable security deposit applies only to motor scooter rentals.
- Same-day rentals depend on stock availability.
- Customers can book and ask for local route advice through WhatsApp.

## Included equipment

- Inline skates, quad roller skates, skateboards and longboards include a child helmet, wrist guards, knee pads and elbow pads at no extra cost.
- Bike rentals include a child helmet and a lock.
- Motor scooter rentals include two helmets and third-party insurance.
